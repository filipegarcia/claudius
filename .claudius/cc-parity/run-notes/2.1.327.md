## Summary

(Draft — rewritten at the end of the run.) 2.1.327 is another large release. Most of it is permission and sandbox hardening, terminal renderer and dialog polish, cloud and Remote Control, artifacts, mods and VS Code, which reach Claudius through the bundled engine or don't apply to a browser. A read-only audit (six parallel agents, one per surface cluster) checked every entry that lands on a surface Claudius draws itself. It found a dozen places where Claudius has its own copy of the bug or gap; the plan is to fix them on the screens Claudius already has.
